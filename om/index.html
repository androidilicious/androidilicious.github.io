      How it works: a fast pattern matcher (dynamic time warping) spots anything that might be a chant. Each candidate is passed to the neural network, a 1-D convolutional network over your voice's spectrogram, which only counts it if it's the full mantra in the right order. Besides your recordings, the network learns from partial and scrambled versions of your own chants.
